MERTEKS — Rubber Seydel

Rubber-coated Seydel cylinders

Rubber coatings that retain no heat on the surface or inner body thanks to proprietary chemical additives, with antistatic and low-friction surfaces. Custom-made as breaking and converter rollers for TWO / NSC / Seydel machines in the acrylic fiber-yarn sector.

MERPOLON — Polyurethane Seydel

Polyurethane-coated Seydel cylinders

Polyurethane Seydel rollers prepared by our specialists in a laboratory environment; they stand out with superior abrasion and tear resistance, heat-dissipating structure, and a hardness (Shore A) value that stays constant throughout operation.
