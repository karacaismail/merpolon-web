Merteks Elastomer

A trusted name in industrial roller coating since 2001.

Merteks Elastomer San. ve Dış Ticaret Ltd. Şti. manufactures rubber and polyurethane coated industrial rollers (cylinders) in the İkitelli Organized Industrial Zone, Istanbul. Under the Merpolon (polyurethane) and Merteks (rubber) brands, we offer a broad range led by Seydel cylinders.

Thanks to proprietary chemical additives, our coatings retain no heat on the surface or inner body and are custom-made as breaking and converter rollers for TWO / NSC / Seydel machines in the acrylic fiber-yarn sector. Our R&D formula prevents static build-up and winding issues; wear resistance is maximal and the rubber does not collapse.

Even after precision grinding, the working life of our cylinders is extended. We produce them with flat, conical and crowned grinding plus diamond and helix channel options to match your operating conditions — with a coating capacity up to Ø1200mm × 7000mm, serving 8 industries.

Quality and trust is our business. Our rubber cylinders carry a 3-month warranty against manufacturing defects.
